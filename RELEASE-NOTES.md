### Search
- **Search your settings:** a new search button at the top of More finds any setting by name.
- **New search options sheet:** the 3-dot on the search bar now holds the search source, engines, number of results and history in one place.
- **Pick a site to search:** choose YouTube, YouTube Music, SoundCloud or Bandcamp.
- **Fewer empty searches:** when yt-dlp finds nothing, NewPipe is tried automatically.
- **Fresh results every time:** searches always run again with the engine you picked, instead of showing old results.
- The search screen has a cleaner header and search field.
- Adding a link that's already in the list now tells you, instead of silently doing nothing.

### Proxy
- **Use a proxy for everything:** HTTP, HTTPS and SOCKS5, for downloads, search, playback and updates.
- If the proxy can't be reached, Hazel shows an error and never connects without it.
- Backups keep your proxy settings without password.
