### Player
- **Feat: Play downloads in Hazel:** audio opens a music sheet with cover art, a wavy seek bar and 10-second skips; video plays full screen or in a sheet.
- **Feat: File actions in the player:** Open with, Show file, Share and Properties, one tap away while it plays.
- **Feat: Keep playing in background:** Home's player plays on with lock-screen and notification controls. Off by default; turn it on in Playback settings or Search options.
- **Feat: Open files with Hazel:** Hazel appears in Android's Open with list for any audio or video file on your phone.

### Gallery
- **Feat: Long press a file** to play or view it full screen and download it, plus open it and see its properties once saved.
- **Fixed: Pinterest videos play in the viewer.**
- **Fixed: Gallery no longer crashes on Android 7.**

### Downloads
- **Feat: Properties show what the file carries:** description, link, artist, genre and upload date written into a video or audio file.
- **Fixed: Facebook "No video found":** a download that fails with saved sign-ins is tried once more without them.

### Share
- **Fixed: Hazel Gallery:** tapping an image in Download all opens its own sheet instead of drawing over the list.
