### Gallery
- **Stories, highlights and profile sections:** an Instagram or X profile link asks what to fetch, two at a time: stories, highlights, posts, reels or tagged on Instagram; media, posts and replies, or likes on X.
- **Videos play in Hazel's own player** in the viewer, with a seek bar and mute; saved or expired links are read again before Play.
- **Save gallery videos your way:** a video, or each video in Download all, opens the video download sheet, so it can be saved as audio or any quality next to the images.
- Several pasted links are read in one pass, keep their order, and a link that fails is reported.
- The image sheet and Download all remember their settings; saved results reopen at once.

### Share
- **Hazel Gallery and Instant Gallery** replace Hazel Images and Instant Images. Share a profile to pick its sections; Instant Gallery asks once, then saves.
- Gallery shares fill their sheet in place, with a loading view that matches it.
- A shared link that can't be read waits in the Failed tab with its log; Retry opens its sheet again.
- A profile Instagram turns away says it may be too many requests: wait, or sign in again.

### Search
- **Media | Gallery switch:** one switch picks the engine, and video sources and image sites share one chip style.
- Image search on Pinterest, Bluesky, Tumblr and DeviantArt.
- An "i" button opens a guide to what each side finds.
- Search opens on Media from Home and on Gallery from the Gallery tab.

### Looks
- **Choose the app icon:** Classic, White or Glass, for the home screen and all four share targets.
- **Five new glass styles:** Rose, Ember, Lagoon, Dusk and Sand, right after Mono.
- Tonal buttons, chips and tabs are see-through on Glass and calmer on Dark.
- The bottom bar shows a cookie or a clover under the chosen tab, with a spring on tap.
- Teal moves up in the accent list; choice dialogs mark the pick with a cookie check.

### Sign-in
- **Quick sign-in card** on the Cookies screen, plus export of chosen accounts to the clipboard or a cookie file, and delete.
- Cookie import reads JSON exports from Cookie-Editor and similar.
- Sign-in pages no longer load blank; they fit, zoom and stay above the keyboard.
- A saved sign-in a site turns away now asks you to sign in again.

### Fixes
- The video download sheet greys out options a plain file link can't use, with a short reason.
- The search bar shows Fetching with a count while several links are read.
- Delete all cookies also clears saved site data and gallery-dl sessions.
