### Images
- **New Gallery tab powered by gallery-dl:** save photos, videos and captions from posts, profiles and galleries on Instagram, X, Reddit, Pinterest, Tumblr and many more sites.
- **Image search across seven sites:** Pinterest, X, Bluesky, Tumblr, DeviantArt, Instagram and Pixiv, with an optional Group by author.
- **Fullscreen image viewer:** swipe, zoom and save each file; posts with several files show as carousels with Download all.
- **Captions:** view, copy, save as text, or write them into saved images.
- Share a post to **Hazel Gallery** to pick what to save, or **Instant Gallery** to save it at once.
- Image downloads use the same Queue, History, Failed tab and sign-in as videos.
- Image settings, a separate Gallery save folder, and gallery-dl updates under Software update.

### Updates
- **Stable, Beta and Nightly channels:** switch in place under Software update, Hazel.
- Optional notification for new versions and background download, on Wi-Fi only if you like.

### Downloads
- **Trim after download:** save the whole file at full speed and cut it on your phone, next to Quick cut and Precise cuts.
- **Save location screen:** your last five folders one tap away.
- A live card on the Downloads screen for the download in progress.

### Sign-in
- **One-tap sign-in for six sites:** Instagram, YouTube, X, Facebook, TikTok and Reddit.
- Account cards show whether each sign-in is active, expired or signed out.
- The cookie option in download sheets shows the site's sign-in state.

### Looks
- Connected M3 groups across settings, the download sheet and the player.
- Calm empty states for Home and Queue.
- New accents: Blush, Powder, Sage and Peach.
- Format badges in crimson, and Mono first among glass styles.

### Faster
- Home opens quickly with large restored results, and every screen opens smoothly the first time.
- Less redrawing while downloading.

- The mini player keeps playing on every screen.
