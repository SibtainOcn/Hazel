Hazel is a downloader for video and audio, built on yt-dlp. Paste a link and it works out what is behind it, whether that is one video, a playlist or an entire channel.

No account is needed, nothing is tracked, and nothing about what you download is sent anywhere.

Downloading

* Works with YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, X, Reddit, SoundCloud and over 1000 other sites
* Paste one link, several at once, or a whole playlist or channel and queue them together
* Hazel Instant: share a link from any other app and the download starts straight away, at a quality you set once
* Downloads keep going in the background, and can be paused, resumed or cancelled from the card or the notification
* Wi-Fi only mode, checked when a download starts so a transfer already running is never cut off

Choosing what you get

* Pick from the formats and containers the source actually offers
* Choose a specific soundtrack where a source publishes more than one
* Edit the title, author, file type and metadata before anything is saved
* Save in any language the source provides

Processing

* SponsorBlock: cut out sponsors, intros and other segments
* Chapters: embed them in the file, or split it into one file per chapter
* Subtitles: burn them in, save them alongside, or both, in the languages you choose
* An offline video to audio converter

Privacy

* Incognito mode: downloads are left out of your library and links are not remembered
* No accounts, no analytics, and no data sent anywhere

Settings

* Dark and light themes with an accent colour of your choosing
* Available in English, Spanish, Hindi, Simplified Chinese, Brazilian Portuguese, French, German, Russian, Japanese and Indonesian, or whichever of those your device is already set to
* The yt-dlp engine updates on its own, from the Stable, Nightly or Master channel
