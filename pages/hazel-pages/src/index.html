01 / Getting started

Three easy ways to save something.

However you found it, there is a short way from there to a file on your phone.

Paste

A link, a few, or a whole playlist

Copy a link from anywhere and paste it in. A playlist or a channel works too, and you can save every video in it with one tap.

youtube.com/playlist?list=PLx8Hj325 videos
Search

Just type what you want

Search YouTube, YouTube Music, SoundCloud, Bandcamp and more right from the home screen, and play any result before you save it.

lofi beats to study to
Share

Hazel Instant

Watching something in another app? Tap Share and pick Hazel. A download sheet pops up right there, set to your usual quality. Tap Download and keep watching.

04 / Privacy

Your phone talks to the site you asked for. That's all.

Hazel has no servers of its own and nothing to report back to. What you save goes from the website straight to your phone.

  • 01
    No accountNothing to sign up for, nothing to sign in to.
  • 02
    No ads, no trackingNo analytics or trackers anywhere in the app.
  • 03
    Incognito modeSaved files stay out of your library and links aren't remembered.
  • 04
    Proxy supportSend every connection through your own HTTP or SOCKS proxy.

05 / Install

Up and running in a minute.

Hazel isn't on the Play Store. Get it from one of the two official places below, and nowhere else.

  1. 01
    Download Hazel

    From GitHub Releases or F-Droid. On GitHub, the file ending in arm64-v8a suits almost every phone.

  2. 02
    Open the file to install

    Your phone may ask you to allow installs from your browser or file manager the first time. That's normal for apps outside the Play Store.

  3. 03
    Paste, search or share

    Open Hazel and paste a link, or share one to Hazel from any app. The guide covers the rest.

Stay safe

Make sure it's the real Hazel

Every official release is signed by the developer. If you got Hazel somewhere else, an app like AppVerifier can compare your copy with the fingerprint below. If they don't match, uninstall it.
